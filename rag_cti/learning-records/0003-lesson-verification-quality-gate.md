# Lesson Verification Quality Gate

The user wants lesson verification to follow the teaching notes and preferences, not only mechanical HTML checks.

Future lesson delivery should include:

- A TDD-style behavior gate: verify observable learner outcomes such as global positioning, concept-first goals, adequate practice, explanatory feedback, and durable review links.
- A grill-with-docs pass: ask whether the lesson is truly written from the user's current knowledge state, whether code has become the primary curriculum again, and whether adjacent concepts are separated clearly enough.
- Documentation updates when a new teaching preference or concept boundary is clarified.

Mechanical checks such as quiz counts, feedback-template counts, links, and whitespace remain necessary, but they are not sufficient.
